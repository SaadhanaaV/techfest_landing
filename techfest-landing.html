Asia's largest science & tech festival

Every December, Techfest turns IIT Bombay's campus into a working laboratory — part competition ground, part exhibition hall, part concert venue.

What started as a college fest now pulls in students from over 2,500 colleges and 60 countries, competing for a prize pool that runs past ₹50 lakh.
